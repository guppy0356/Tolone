# Book Shelf

A shelf for the books you bought, each with a short review you can share.

## Requirements

- Register a book
  - [ ] registers a book from its ISBN-13
- My books
  - [ ] shows the books I registered
  - [ ] shows each book's cover, title, authors and publisher
  - [ ] leaves archived books out of the results
  - [ ] filters the books by title
  - [ ] filters the books by registered date
  - [ ] filters the books by publisher
  - [ ] filters the books by author
  - [ ] sorts the books by title
  - [ ] pages through the books
- Book detail
  - [ ] archives the book instead of deleting it
  - [ ] writes a short review of the book
  - [ ] updates the review later
  - [ ] shares the review
  - [ ] deletes the review
- Shared reviews
  - [ ] shows the shared reviews
  - [ ] shows each shared review's book, who wrote it and the review
  - [ ] filters the shared reviews by who wrote them
  - [ ] filters the shared reviews by posted date
  - [ ] sorts the shared reviews by title
  - [ ] pages through the shared reviews
